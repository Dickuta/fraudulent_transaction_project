## Business Challenge
Digital payment platforms face increasing fraud, financial losses, false positives, and regulatory pressure. Traditional rule-based and manual detection methods struggle to scale and adapt to evolving fraud tactics, particularly with highly imbalanced transaction data where fraud accounts for less than 1% of transactions.

There is therefore a need for an adaptive, scalable, and explainable machine-learning solution that can detect fraudulent transactions accurately while minimising false positives and supporting AML/KYC compliance.
